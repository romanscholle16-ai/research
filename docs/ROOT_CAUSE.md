# Root cause: why ~195k experiments produced no qualified strategy

Evidence: `docs/FORENSICS.md` (generated by `scripts/forensics.py` from the live DB), `docs/LEVERAGE_SWEEP.md`, counterfactual diagnoses (`failure_diagnoses` table).

## Findings
1. **Search volume was not the bottleneck.** 195k experiment rows covered 77,486 distinct (entry logic, symbol, timeframe) hypotheses (51.5% of rows are exit-only re-tests of an
   entry already tried). 60,830 of them are GP rules. The registry blocked a further 3.77M generated candidates (3.2M `exit_variant_limit`, 0.57M `noise_neighbourhood`) – generation
   is cheap but the director kept proposing capped entries; this was fixed earlier with exhausted-arm tracking (work now goes to unexplored cells).
2. **Funnel shape.** 52.6% of rows die at eligibility (pre-signal frequency/cost/economics), 20.8% at screen (`no_edge`), 3,989 reached validation-pass, 3,982 reached the locked test and
   **all 3,982 failed `cannot_reach_target_test`** (plus `fails_multiple_testing` 3,933, `inadequate_trade_frequency` 3,615). The failure is arithmetic: dev-validated edges are
   +0.05…+0.20R per trade; with risk ≤10% of equity and leverage ≤50x that compounds far below $1,000/month from $300.
3. **Where the surviving edge lives.** Counterfactual diagnosis of the best dev-validated rules (ETH/SOL/BTC/BNB 15m–1h `gp_rule`): all are **long-only, 6-ATR stops (3–11% stop distance)**,
   gross edge ≈ net edge (cost is not their problem: +0.21R gross vs +0.18R net), positive in up-markets and weaker/negative in flat/down markets (BNB: up +0.145R vs down −0.059R). This is
   market beta + trend persistence, not a leverage-scalable scalping edge.
4. **Leverage incompatibility (measured).** `docs/LEVERAGE_SWEEP.md`: for those rules, every path is liquidated at ≥25x (liq-paths 100%), median wealth falls below $300 for SOL at 25x/50x, and at
   100x+ the strategy cannot be sized at all. Liquidation distance = 1/L − mmr: 250x ⇒ 0.30%, 500x ⇒ 0.10%; a 6-ATR stop of 3–11% needs ≤ ~9–25x.
   Therefore a 250x/500x strategy needs a *different* edge: stops ≤0.24% (≈0.5–1 ATR on 5m), wins as multiples of R. That is what the new `user_500x_maker` epoch tests.
5. **No gate bug found.** Gates were audited on representative records; rejections reproduce. One real defect was found and fixed: two multi-day archive gaps (2022-03-01 +72h and 2022-04-03 +48h in
   SOL/XRP) – backtests could hold positions across them; positions are now force-closed before any >12h outage and the data agent reports the gaps.
6. **Cost model was pre-judging the 250x/500x case.** Under market-order costs the stop window at 250x is empty (cost ≥0.6R at a 0.24% stop). Passive limit execution (maker entries/targets, taker stops)
   shrinks round-trip cost to ~0.10% and opens the window; believability priors (cost ≤1.5R, gross edge ≤2R) were widened for this mode only. Backtest/OOS/locked-test gates are unchanged.

## What changed because of this
- Forensics agent (counterfactuals → named, evidence-cited causes → hints) feeds the repair agent; repair is skipped (archived) when gross edge ≈ 0.
- Data-integrity agent audits every dataset every 6h and quarantines hard failures; the director never plans quarantined cells.
- High-leverage epoch (5m/15m/1h; stop grid down to 0.3 ATR; rr up to 6) is running; `scripts/leverage_sweep.py` reports where leverage helps or hurts.
